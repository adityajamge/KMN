What a morning looks like

  1. Arrive and settle · 6:30 – 7:00, register and leave the rush at the door; we begin at 7:00 sharp
  2. Understand · the scientific and spiritual reasoning behind the mind, the brain and their link with the body
  3. Practise · the same morning, live it through Shadanga Kriya: core meditative practice with sound frequencies
  4. Settle into clarity · come back slowly, with clarity and a realisation about your own life
  5. Integrate · carry the patterns and learning into your own day, in time for school or work

After the program

After Day 4, you decide.

No pressure, and no follow-up calls you didn't ask for. If you want to continue, move to the Comprehensive program and your ₹{{corePrice}} comes off the ₹{{fullPrice}} fee. If not, you keep the breathing and relaxation routine you learned, and you can keep practising on your own.

30 days build the practice. 90 days make it yours.

  • 30 guided mornings at the centre, then 90 days of guided self-practice with the Shadanga Kriya app
  • Weekend sessions at the centre for doubt-clearing and community gathering
  • A weekly progress check by the team

Why 120 days: a new habit takes about 66 days on average to feel automatic (Lally et al., 2009), so 30 days alone isn't enough.

It's not for you if you

  • Want a guru or a set of beliefs to follow
  • Expect instant results without daily practice
  • Want it to replace medical or psychological treatment. It supports care; it never replaces it.
  • Are below Class 8 (a children's format is being developed)

Your guide, not your guru

It began with one word: "Why?"

Yogee grew up in a traditional Vedic family and wouldn't follow any practice he couldn't understand. That question took him back into the Vedas, the Upanishads and Patanjali, then into neuroscience, then across India to learn from yogis and scientists.

After 20 years of practice he built Shadanga Kriya, so that no one has to depend on anyone to find their way, including him.
